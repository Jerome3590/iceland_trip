## Phase 4 — Deep South (May 11)
**Base: Hvolsvöllur**

## May 11 — Big East Run

**5:30am — Leave Hvolsvöllur**

**~6:00am — Urriðafoss**
Quick roadside stop on Route 1, Iceland's highest volume waterfall, 15 min

**~9:00am — Jökulsárlón Glacier Lagoon**
45 min — icebergs, seals, beat the crowds completely

**~10:00am — Diamond Beach**
30–40 min — ice chunks on black sand, right across the road from the lagoon

**~10:45am — Head back west on Route 1**

**~1:00pm — Reynisfjara Black Sand Beach**
45 min — basalt columns, Reynisdrangar sea stacks, dramatic waves

**~2:00pm — Skógafoss**
Hike up the Fimmvörðuháls trail along the Skógá river, 60 min
20+ waterfalls back to back
Duck behind **Kvernufoss** on the way back — hidden, most tourists miss it

**~4:00pm — Drive back to Hvolsvöllur**
45 min

**~4:45pm — Back at Hvolsvöllur**

## May 14 — Reykjavík City Day

### Must Do
- **National Museum of Iceland** — best single museum in the country
- **Perlan** — planetarium, ice cave exhibit, 360 views
- **Settlement Exhibition** — Viking-age ruins under glass in the city center
- **Laugavegur** — main shopping and café street
- **Tjörnin Pond** — easy morning walk

### If Time Allows
- **Kolaportið Flea Market** — weekends only, local goods and food
- **Nauthólsvík Geothermal Beach** — weather dependent
- **Live jazz/blues** — check Harpa and Húrra for listings
